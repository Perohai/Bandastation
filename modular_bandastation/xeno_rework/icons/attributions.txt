BandaStation artwork
-------------------
Original module assets were made for BandaStation with OpenAI image generation
and local sprite packing or pixel edits. License: CC BY-SA 3.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

  items/{resin_fruits,fruit_inhands}.dmi, actions/hive_abilities.dmi,
  structures/{biomass_generator,hive_fortifications,hive_core,brood_core}.dmi,
  and effects/{xeno_effects,rally_track}.dmi.

The generator concept used a StarCraft Extractor design reference (Blizzard
Entertainment). The final atlas was made for this module.
The beetle, mantis and scorpion atlases use TGMC references listed below and
retain the donor artwork restrictions.

castes/big_xenos.dmi, actions/xeno_actions.dmi and structures/resin_door.dmi
come from BandaStation PR #1969 by Perohai (Artfish). Original modpack credits:
Artfish, Paxilmaniac.
The resin door was moved with its pixels intact.
https://github.com/ss220club/BandaStation/pull/1969
