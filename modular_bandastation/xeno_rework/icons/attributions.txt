BandaStation artwork
-------------------
Original module assets were made for BandaStation with OpenAI image generation
and local sprite packing or pixel edits. License: CC BY-SA 3.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

  resin_fruits.dmi, fruit_inhands.dmi, new_actions.dmi, combat_actions.dmi,
  xeno_effects.dmi, hive_actions.dmi, brood_actions.dmi, support_actions.dmi,
  biomass_generator.dmi, hive_fortifications.dmi,
  brood/{hive_core,brood_core}.dmi and rally_track.dmi.

The generator concept used a StarCraft Extractor design reference (Blizzard
Entertainment). The final atlas was made for this module.
The beetle, mantis and scorpion atlases use TGMC references listed below and
retain the donor artwork restrictions.

big_xenos.dmi, xeno_actions.dmi and resin_door.dmi come from BandaStation PR
#1969 by Perohai (Artfish). Original modpack credits: Artfish, Paxilmaniac.
The resin door was moved with its pixels intact.
https://github.com/ss220club/BandaStation/pull/1969

TGMC artwork
------------
Author: TerraGov Marine Corps contributors. Original restrictions, including
applicable CC BY-NC and CC BY-NC-SA terms and per-asset exceptions, apply to
source files and derivatives. The upstream README links 4.0; the bundled
upstream license texts are labelled 3.0 and are retained with the inputs.
https://github.com/tgstation/TerraGov-Marine-Corps#licensing

  Final castes and actions: icons/Xeno/castes and icons/Xeno/actions at
  df1917855472062520be96d592f88e3973efefbe. Local *_castes.dmi and *_actions.dmi
  retain original pixels. *_adapted.dmi uses renamed states, resized or padded
  frames and selected poses.
  https://github.com/tgstation/TerraGov-Marine-Corps/tree/df1917855472062520be96d592f88e3973efefbe/icons/Xeno

  Brood and Carrier: icons/Xeno/castes/{beetle,mantis,scorpion,baneling,carrier}.dmi
  at 94bbe4ce7716cebedda1d0c36968c07eda62ad01. Originals and hashes are in
  tgmc/minions_source/. brood/baneling.dmi crops and scales native frames.
  tgmc/carrier_adapted.dmi renames states and preserves clinger overlays.
  https://github.com/tgstation/TerraGov-Marine-Corps/tree/94bbe4ce7716cebedda1d0c36968c07eda62ad01/icons/Xeno/castes

  Hivemind: icons/Xeno/castes/hivemind.dmi and icons/Xeno/actions/hivemind.dmi at
  2b7bb78c00425676035d3fdaabc598d3ce112534. Originals, hashes and license texts
  are in tgmc/hivemind_source/. The related DM logic is adapted under AGPL-3.0.
  https://github.com/tgstation/TerraGov-Marine-Corps/tree/2b7bb78c00425676035d3fdaabc598d3ce112534

  Folding barricade: icons/Marine/barricades.dmi. Archive revision unknown.
  The byte-identical source and its SHA-256 are in fortifications/source/.
  The gameplay atlas retains the original folding_0 through folding_3 frames.
  https://github.com/tgstation/TerraGov-Marine-Corps
